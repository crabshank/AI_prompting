For all recipes, use the concept of 'active time':{

Time spent in the kitchen (excluding cleanup, washing up, unmonitored cooking time), allowing for paralellisation of operations, e.g.: Boiling water, whilst a pot simmers, while chopping an onion during a 5 minute period counts as 5 minutes of active time.
}
I prefer to minimise active time when cooking.


When considering microwaving things: consider the power used (adjusting the power the microwave uses); e.g. microwaving chocolate at full power is too aggressive for meting, or microwaving porridge at full power may cause the porridge to overflow its bowl.
